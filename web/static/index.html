📖 How to Play

🎯 Game Objective

Be the first player to move one of your pieces to the opposite side of the board.

🎲 Basic Rules

  • Movement: Click your piece, then click a highlighted square to move
  • Colors: When you move to a tile, your piece takes on that tile's color
  • Turn-based: Players alternate turns
  • Winning: Get any piece to the opponent's starting row

✨ Skill Mode

Each color grants special abilities based on how many pieces of that color you have:

  • Move Extended: Move extra squares (2-4 based on intensity)
  • Move Opponent: Force opponent pieces to move
  • Block Tile: Temporarily block tiles (1-3 rounds)

Intensity increases with more pieces of the same color (1x, 2x, 3x)

💡 Strategy Tips

  • Plan your path to collect colors that give you useful abilities
  • Block opponent paths with Block Tile skill
  • Use Move Extended to rush toward the goal
  • Move Opponent can disrupt enemy strategies
